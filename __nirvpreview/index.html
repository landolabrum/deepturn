Why storage

The grid is the single point of failure.

Solar alone stops the moment the utility does. Storage is what turns an array into power you can count on — through an outage, a rate hike, or a property the line never reached.

  • Ride through outages

    Critical circuits — well pump, refrigeration, HVAC, medical equipment — transfer over automatically and keep running.

  • Use what you generate

    Store the midday surplus instead of exporting it, and draw it back down after sunset when utility rates are at their worst.

  • Build where there is no line

    A properly sized off-grid system removes the utility extension quote from the equation entirely on remote Arizona land.
